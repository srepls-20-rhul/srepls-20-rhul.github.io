About S-REPLS

Twenty meetings of a South-of-England PL community

S-REPLS is a regular, informal meeting for anyone in the South of England with a professional interest — academic or commercial — in programming language theory, semantics, and implementation. It moves around the region from host to host and is always free to attend.

For our twentieth edition, S-REPLS comes to Royal Holloway, University of London, hosted by the Department of Computing, Security and Mathematics, and supported by the EPSRC-funded project INDIMO.

Call for talks

Call for talks

The programme for S-REPLS 20 has not yet been finalised. As always, talks can cover any aspect of programming language theory, design, or implementation. PhD students and first-time speakers are welcome, as are talks on work in progress.

🎤

Any career stage

PhD students, postdocs, academics and industry researchers are all welcome to offer a talk.

⏱️

Format

Slots are typically 20–30 minutes including questions; shorter talks can also be accommodated.

🧪

Work in progress

Talks need not present finished results — work in progress is a normal part of the programme.
